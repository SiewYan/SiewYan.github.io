Why the energy cut makes a wiggle

In the muon rest frame, energetic positrons preferentially follow the spin. A boost along the muon momentum turns that energy–angle correlation into a spin-dependent fraction above a lab energy threshold. The fully inclusive ideal decay population has no wiggle.

d²P / dx dcosθ = x² [(3 − 2x) + P cosθ (2x − 1)]

Massless electron, leading order; radiative corrections omitted. Only the positron and combined invisible recoil are modeled. Neutrinos never hit a detector.

A HIT IS A MEASUREMENT

Three instruments. Different jobs.

Calorimeters measure positron energy and arrival time. Trackers constrain decay geometry. Field probes provide the independent magnetic measurement.

What acceptance means

Ideal benchmark: all decays within the time window are accepted, with exact energy and a declared 20 ns time-of-flight offset. Random station assignment is illustrative.

Fast response: 75% independent efficiency, toy smearing, the same 20 ns flight model, illustrative stations.

Geometric: analytic helical trajectories intersect finite calorimeter surfaces; some positrons are undetected. Hit coordinates never move when the display is enlarged.

Instrument and orbit boundaries

The vacuum chamber, inflector, kicker, magnet returns, focusing electrodes and collimators are schematic apparatus representations. The quantitative transport uses a uniform vertical B field with no electric field and no vertical beam motion.

Off-magic momentum is therefore a pure-B lesson. Electric focusing and pitch corrections are described qualitatively, not transported.

Magic-momentum condition
aμ − 1 / (γ²magic − 1) = 0

This cancels the ideal transverse electric-field contribution to anomalous precession. It does not remove electric focusing from the orbit or eliminate real experimental corrections.

Fit method, bounds & correlations

Binned Poisson likelihood with exact integrated bin expectations, including empty bins. N₀, τ, A, ω and φ are fitted; N₀ is analytically profiled. Broad frequency search 0.2–3.5 rad/μs, ten separated starts. Bounds: 10 < τ < 200 μs, 0 ≤ A < 0.95. Covariance uses observed likelihood curvature. Nonpositive curvature, competing aliases, sparse samples and weak modulation are rejected.

Measured arrival times are fitted. The fixed 20 ns flight delay in ideal/fast mode is absorbed by fitted phase and normalization for windows starting after the delay. Geometry-dependent flight, momentum spread and gain drift can cause model mismatch.

Residuals

(Observed − expected) / √expected. The fit uses Poisson likelihood, not these residuals as Gaussian input.

Phase-folded arrivals

Raw selected counts folded by fitted phase, with the integrated fit folded into the same bins.

THE NECESSARY SECOND MEASUREMENT

Measure the field independently.

48 toy probes observe a uniform field in tesla. Their random stream is separate from the positron stream. A common calibration offset affects all probes together.

RING → FREQUENCY → FIELD → RESULT

The magnetic anomaly.

aμ = mμ ωa / (|q| Bmeasured)
Fit the positron arrivals and take independent field observations to extract the anomaly.

Changing the calibration correction reuses the field readings and holds the positron sample fixed. A common uncertainty does not average down with more probes or muons.

The complete g factor

Muon mass and elementary charge are externally supplied constants. The simulation measures ωa from arrival times and B from independent probe readings. This is a direct-tesla toy calibration; the real experiment uses proton references, calibration transfers, beam weighting and corrections.

Generation physics & detector assumptions

Efficiency factor: 1 − d exp(−t/80 μs), independently switchable from gain: 1 + h exp(−t/80 μs). The efficiency correction is exact for ideal acceptance with fixed flight time. Gain drift has no supplied corrected fit; inspect and report its model mismatch. No pile-up, CBO or material interactions are implemented.

A generation change starts a new dataset. Analysis cuts reuse existing events. Fills contain up to 1,000 generated muons at a common t = 0; bunch-time spread is zero. Fixed ±45 mm radial acceptance is a simplified storage cut, not a calculated injection efficiency.
