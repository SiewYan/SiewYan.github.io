Associate Professor of Physics · Xiamen University Malaysia

Hoh Siew Yan 何守仁

I am an experimental particle physicist. My work runs from Higgs boson measurements with CMS at CERN to the Muon g−2 storage ring at Fermilab and the muon EDM search at PSI. At XMUM I lead the high energy physics group and am developing muon radiography for Malaysia’s East Coast Rail Link.
